# Requirement Brief

> 建议存放路径：`.pm-workspace/analysis/<项目名>/`

## Request

What was asked for, in the user's own words?

## Business Goal

What outcome does the requester actually want?

## Target Users

Who is affected most directly?

## Current State

What happens today?

## Desired Change

What should be different after this work ships?

## Scope

What is in scope right now?

## Non-Goals

What should stay out of scope for this round?

## Reference Projects

Which prior projects, screens, or decisions are worth checking?

## Constraints

What technical, timeline, compliance, or staffing constraints matter?

## Open Questions

What still needs stakeholder confirmation?

## Risks

What might go wrong if we interpret the request incorrectly?

## Recommended Next Step

What should happen next: clarify, research, prototype, PRD, or story writing?
